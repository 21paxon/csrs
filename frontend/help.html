CSRS Help Guide

Learn how to use the system and the actions available to your role.

Welcome to CSRS

Use this guide to report campus safety concerns, follow your work, and find the tools available to your account.

Your current role: Visitor

Sign in to open your dashboard. Your account role controls which protected actions and records you can access.

Getting started

  1. Sign in with your username, email, or registration number.
  2. Use the navigation menu to open the sections available to your role.
  3. Open My Profile to check your account details or set up two-factor sign-in.
  4. Use Change Password if you need to update your password.

Report and follow up

  1. Open Incidents or My Reports and choose the report action.
  2. Choose the closest category and campus location, then describe what happened clearly.
  3. Submit the report and use its status or reference to follow up.
  4. For an immediate threat, use the Emergency section and contact campus security.

Student and staff tools

  • Use My Reports to see reports linked to your account.
  • Use the emergency centre to request urgent help and provide your location.
  • In Lost and Found, report property you lost and review found-item notices.

Security officer tools

  • In Visitor Management, register visitors, select their host, and check them in.
  • Use the visitor list to check a visitor out when they leave campus.
  • In Security Patrols, enter the checkpoint QR code and add any observation.
  • Review and respond to campus emergency alerts assigned to security.

Head of Security tools

  • Review and coordinate incident response, assign officers, and update case status.
  • Manage campus visitors and review gate activity.
  • Create patrol routes, add checkpoints, assign duty shifts, and review scans.
  • Review emergency alerts, reports, found property, and audit activity available to your role.

College management tools

  • Use the dashboard to review campus safety activity available to management.
  • Open Reports for operational summaries and follow up with the responsible security team.
  • Use emergency channels for urgent matters rather than waiting for a report review.

Dean tools

  • Review student incident activity and reports available to the Dean role.
  • Use report summaries to follow cases with the security team.
  • Direct immediate emergencies to the campus emergency centre.

Administrator tools

  • In User Management, approve accounts, activate or deactivate users, and assign the role matching their college duties.
  • Role assignment automatically gives the actions permitted by that role. Users must sign in again after a role change.
  • Use System Settings for college contact details and registration access.
  • Review audit logs and reports for system activity.

When something does not work

  • Refresh the section and check your internet connection.
  • If access is denied, ask an administrator to confirm your account role and status.
  • If your role was just changed, sign out and sign in again.
  • For urgent safety issues, contact campus security directly.
